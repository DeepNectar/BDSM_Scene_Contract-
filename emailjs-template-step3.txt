-----------------------------------------------------
WHY YOU GOT "Variables size limit ... 50Kb"
-----------------------------------------------------
EmailJS counts the TOTAL size of ALL variables combined.
Older app versions sent the same contract THREE times
(message + message_html + message_text) which tripled the
size and tripped the limit. v4.30 sends ONE variable only:
{{message}} — verified ~5.5 KB in tests. Update the app to
v4.30 (deploy js/app.js + index.html + sw.js) and keep the
template body exactly as in STEP B above.
